V14.0.4 · Nisekyu!! Thanksgiving Special

November 26, 2026

Four new 3★ Bonyari High cards

  • Chitoge Kirisaki — Fake Sweetheart: a Pow attacker who uses a Key for guaranteed follow-ups and to ignore percentage damage reduction.
  • Seishiro Tsugumi — Black Tiger: steps in to protect Chitoge or a keyed ally once per turn, then strengthens the protected ally.
  • Kosaki Onodera — Sweet Promise: distributes Keys and heals the team.
  • Marika Tachibana — Determined Fiancée: receives attacks, grants Keys at low team HP, and halves pre-combat damage to the team.

Each card has one shiny artwork. A Key grants Pow/Qik+3 during combat and is consumed after its holder initiates combat. Each unit can hold one; a team can hold three. Cleanses do not remove Keys.

Nisekyu!! Pack · 500 coins · 10 cards

Use ;pack nisekyu. Each card rolls independently: 25% Bonyari High, 50% returning Nisekyu cast, 25% other pullable characters. Duplicates are possible. Regular pity is unchanged.

Within the returning cast, 60% goes to Tanaka, Nishinoya, Hinata — Miracle Maker, and Kageyama — King of the Lost Court; 40% goes to Hinata — Wings of Dawn and Kageyama — Crown of Precision / Prodigious Talent. Each group is split equally.

The sale lasts 96 hours from the bot’s first startup with this release. The saved deadline survives restarts; ;shop and ;info p nisekyu show it. Bonyari cards are exclusive to this pack and cannot be crafted or pulled from regular packs. Their character-shard drops stop at the same deadline.

Tiny Titans · Raku’s Locket

Face the Bonyari High Promise Challenge to earn locket shards. The locket doubles the current Bonyari school bond without stacking. At the start of each Bonyari unit’s action, its wearer has a 2/3 chance to cleanse itself.

Craft with 25 locket shards + 25 character shards from any combination of Bonyari cards + 500 coins. All four Bonyari cards can equip it. Recycle value: 100 Clan Dollars. The challenge and recipe remain available after the pack sale ends.

V14.0.2 · Balance and command update

November 23, 2026

Tiny Titans PvP commands

Use ;pvptt to play, ;ttboosts to see this week’s boosted units, and ;pvpttboard or ;ttboard for the leaderboard. The previous spellings remain aliases. Existing Elo, rewards and team settings carry over.

Semi — Pinch Ace

  • Base Qik: 53 → 52.
  • Lightning Serve: pre-combat damage scaling reduced from 0.8× to 0.7×.

Hirugami — The Great Wall (5★)

  • Wall allies: front-line MBs, or front-line allies whose Def is at least 5 higher than Rec. A unit with 29 Rec needs 34 Def. Back-line units do not qualify.
  • The Gate Holds: Wall allies gain Pow/Qik equal to 10% of Def, down from 20%.
  • Flat damage reduction is now 15% of Def, down from 20%. When another flat reduction applies, it uses 7.5%, down from 10%. Both cases are capped at 8 per hit.
  • Percentages round down: the reduction reaches its cap at 54 Def normally, or 107 Def with another flat reduction.
  • Hold the Net: start-of-turn Pow/Qik/Def and Spd/Jmp bonuses reduced from +3 to +2.
  • End-of-turn healing reduced from 3 to 2 HP per MB, including Hirugami. Caps are now 6 in 3v3, 12 in 6v6, and 18 in 9v9.

V14.0.0 · Team builds and Tiny Titans PvP

November 22, 2026

PvP matchmaking

  • ;pvp works through a shuffled daily queue of eligible opponents. Players you have already faced go behind fresh opponents. After the queue pass, it picks a random unrated match. A later rated result can requeue a newly eligible opponent at the rear.
  • If you won a pair’s last rated match today, your next challenge against them is unrated. If they beat you in a rated rematch, you can earn or lose Elo against them again. Unrated results never change that record.
  • Eligibility resets at midnight in the game timezone. Results are labelled Rated or Unrated.

Tiny Titans PvP

  • ;pvpttb [@player] uses its own Elo board, daily matchup records and coin allowance. Use ;pvpttbboard for standings.
  • Four cards from each rarity 1★–4★ receive the normal Tiny Titans stat boost each week. ;ttbboosts lists the picks. 5★ and Duo cards are never boosted, and no card is selected two weeks in a row.
  • Weekly rewards stack: everyone eligible gets 100 Clan Dollars, with another 200/300/400/500 for the top 80%/60%/40%/20%. The top bracket earns 1,500 total. Play a rated match in the current two-week season to qualify.
  • Boosts and weekly rewards change at Monday’s cutoff. Elo resets every two weeks, on that same cutoff. Normal PvP retains its existing pack rewards.

Locked team builds

  • Lock a loadout in ;teamedit, ;af1team or ;pvpset to preserve its characters, rows and exact item copies.
  • A character can hold different items in different locked teams. Using the same character and item copy across several teams reserves only one copy. Other characters need their own copies.
  • Reserved items cannot be moved to another character, recycled or traded. Item views show the teams holding each copy.
  • Unlocking previews item changes and returns to current equipped items. An unlocked PvP lineup also refreshes from its source team.

New cards

  • 5★ Hirugami — The Great Wall: supports front-line Wall allies with damage scaling and flat reduction. Monster Block reduces a foe’s hit and reflects the prevented damage after combat, up to 20. Stuff Block adds Heal Block, team damage and a limited cleanse.
  • 1★ Kunimi — Energy Saver: builds Reserve stacks, then spends them on Full Power for a burst of Pow/Spd/Jmp. Spending three stacks prevents counterattacks. Lazy Tip gains extra damage during Full Power.
  • Hirugami — The Immovable Gate is now 4★.

Equipment and smaller changes

  • Fish Cakes: Recovery raid, 2 shards. Protects its wearer from foe effects after combat or outside their own combat. Blocks foe after-combat team damage when the wearer fought, but does not block Save or redirect triggers.
  • Chocolate Milk: Tempo raid, 2 shards. Protects its wearer and same-row allies from foe effects that increase remaining Ultimate cooldown.
  • Aran’s unique item converts positive visible and in-combat Spd bonuses into Jmp at 100%, up from 70%. Visible Spd penalties do not reduce the conversion.
  • Unique equipment follows regular items in ;items and has a blue [Unique] category on the website. ;tv [slot] aliases teamview. Clear Team uses a broom and a grey or blurple button.

V13.0.1 — PvP rematches

November 20, 2026

  • Rematches are allowed. The last-ranked-winner rule now controls Elo eligibility instead of blocking the battle.
  • If you won the pair’s last ranked match, you can still challenge that opponent; the rematch is unrated and changes neither player’s Elo.
  • The losing player can immediately initiate a ranked rematch. Their ranked win restores the other player’s Elo eligibility.
  • Daily coin limits, PvP Shields and valid-team requirements remain unchanged.
