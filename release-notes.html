V13.0.1 — PvP rematches

November 20, 2026

  • Rematches are allowed. The last-ranked-winner rule now controls Elo eligibility instead of blocking the battle.
  • If you won the pair’s last ranked match, you can still challenge that opponent; the rematch is unrated and changes neither player’s Elo.
  • The losing player can immediately initiate a ranked rematch. Their ranked win restores the other player’s Elo eligibility.
  • Daily coin limits, PvP Shields and valid-team requirements remain unchanged.

V13 — Semi & balance update

New 4★: Semi — Pinch Ace

150 HP · 53 Qik · 27 Def · 28 Rec · 36 Spd · 35 Jmp

  • Lightning Serve (CD 4): Before initiating, deals (Qik−foe’s Rec) × 0.8 per foe, minimum 0. Uses pre-combat stats.
  • Talent’s Troubles: Qik/Spd+5 when initiating. After his serve, non-Setter allies gain Pow/Qik+4 for their next combat.
  • Setter’s Magic: Each turn, reduces remaining Ult CD by 1 per allied Setter, including himself; capped at 3.
  • Rightful Rhythm: After combat, refunds 1 Ult CD if his Ultimate triggered.

Find Semi in 4★ sources; ;gallery Semi has his kit and boon/bane advice.
