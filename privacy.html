Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the operator of the Match Point Discord bot, also known as HiQ Battler (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles personal information when you use the bot, its game features, and this website (the “Service”).

Effective and last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Information we process

We receive information from Discord when you interact with the bot, from the actions you take in the game, and from information you voluntarily provide to support. Depending on the features you use, this includes:

  • Discord account information: your Discord user ID, username or display name, and member information made available to the bot. Usernames are stored in some game records, including card provenance, clan membership, and tournament or PvP profiles.
  • Game information: your tutorial status, cards and their ownership history, items, shards, virtual currency, teams and team names, preferences, progression, reward claims, and activity timestamps.
  • Multiplayer and transaction records: clan membership and applications, check-ins, donations, trades, auction listings and bids, opponents, battle or event state and results, scores, ratings, rankings, and rewards.
  • Interaction context: commands and their inputs, button and menu selections, and relevant server, channel, and message IDs used to respond to you or resume game sessions.
  • Support and diagnostics: information you send to maintainers, such as issue descriptions and screenshots, and operational logs or error details generated while the bot runs.

The bot does not request your Discord password, account token, email address, payment details, or government identification. Do not include credentials or unnecessary sensitive information in commands or support requests.

2. Messages and Discord access

The bot uses Discord’s message-content access to recognize and process prefix commands, such as commands beginning with ;, and uses member information for player identification and game interactions. Discord may make messages and member information available to the bot within the permissions and intents it has been granted, including messages that are not commands.

The game database stores command-derived game state rather than a general archive of server conversations. Message content may be processed temporarily in memory, and command inputs or related information may appear in diagnostic errors or information you provide to support.

3. How we use information

We use information to identify your game account, respond to commands, save and restore progress, run multiplayer features and transactions, calculate results and rewards, enforce game limits, diagnose problems, respond to support and privacy requests, prevent exploitation, and comply with applicable law and Discord requirements.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use Discord message content to train artificial intelligence models.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the Service you request under our Terms of Service, legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the Service where those interests do not override your rights, and compliance with legal obligations. If a particular use requires consent, we will obtain it; you may withdraw that consent.

4. Visibility and sharing

Other players: game features can display usernames, player IDs or mentions, card ownership information, teams, clan information, auction activity, scores, rankings, and results to other users. Some features, including the market and PvP leaderboards, operate across servers. Replies in a server channel are visible to people with access to that channel; do not treat them as private. Some interactions use private messages or Discord’s ephemeral responses instead.

Service providers: Discord carries bot interactions and stores messages sent through its platform. The bot runs on hosting infrastructure used by the operator, currently Wispbyte, where game data and runtime logs are processed. GitHub Pages serves this website. These providers process information necessary to deliver their services. See Discord’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for their own practices.

Maintainers and required disclosures: authorized maintainers may access information needed to operate the bot, investigate errors or exploitation, and handle requests. We disclose Discord API data only to service providers as permitted by Discord’s terms, when required by applicable law, or when you expressly direct us to do so, including through the game features you choose to use.

5. Website and browser storage

The website uses your browser’s local storage to remember the light or dark theme and saved character bookmarks. Those preferences remain in that browser and are not uploaded by the website to the bot’s database. The tier maker keeps your list in page memory and resets it when you reload.

We do not add advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers to this website. GitHub Pages may process technical request information, including your IP address, when serving the site, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

You can clear the website’s stored preferences through your browser’s site-data settings. This does not delete your Discord game account. The bot also retrieves character artwork from external image hosts when generating some game images; those requests originate from the bot’s host.

6. Storage and retention

Game records are stored in the bot’s database on the operator’s hosting infrastructure. Account progress and related records persist between sessions; there is currently no general automatic deletion solely because an account becomes inactive. Removing the bot from one server does not itself delete an account that can be used across servers.

We retain personal information only while necessary for the game features and other purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable law and Discord’s requirements. Support and diagnostic information is retained only as needed to resolve the relevant issue and meet applicable obligations. We will promptly delete personal information when it is no longer needed, when the Service stops operating, or upon a valid deletion request, unless retention is legally required.

If personal information exists in backups, deletion requests also cover those copies. Where a backup cannot be edited immediately, we will restrict its use pending removal or replacement and ensure deleted information is not reintroduced into active use if a backup is restored. We will explain any applicable limitation when handling your request.

7. Your choices and data requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact the bot’s maintainers through the official support server. Ask a maintainer for a private way to submit a privacy request, identify the request as a “Privacy request,” and provide your Discord user ID and the action you want us to take. Do not post sensitive information in a public channel. We may verify that you control the relevant Discord account before acting.

We handle requests promptly and within the time limits required by applicable law. Account deletion can permanently remove your collection, teams, balances, and progress. For shared game records, we may remove identifying information while preserving non-identifying results and other players’ records. If information must be retained by law, we will explain the reason and restrict its use accordingly. Using the bot again after deletion may create a new game account.

You may stop using the bot, and authorized server administrators may remove it or change its server permissions. Deleting the bot’s database records does not automatically remove messages or copies controlled by Discord or other users. You may need to use Discord’s controls or contact the relevant server administrators for those copies.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to receive a portable copy of your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us through the same support route to exercise these rights.

8. Security and international processing

We are responsible for applying reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information and for limiting access to people who need it to operate and support the Service. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please report suspected data exposure privately to the maintainers through the support server.

Discord, GitHub, and the bot’s hosting providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Applicable legal requirements for international transfers continue to apply. We will provide notices of a personal-data breach where required by law and Discord’s requirements.

9. Age requirements

The Service is intended only for people who are at least 13 and meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any higher minimum required where they live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who do not meet those requirements. If you believe such information has been provided, contact the maintainers so we can investigate and delete it as required.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Service or its data practices change. We will publish the updated version and date on this page and provide notice of material changes through the website or support server. Where required, we will obtain consent before a new use of personal information.

The operator and maintainer team of Match Point / HiQ Battler is the contact responsible for the practices described here. For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainers through the official support server. Discord’s handling of your account is governed separately by Discord’s Privacy Policy.
